02 // SERVICE HISTORY ARCHIVED / ACTIVE
CANADIAN ARMED FORCES // PRE-FOUNDATION

Cold-Warfare Specialization

Prior to Foundation recruitment, Cardinal underwent Canadian Armed Forces training and specialized in cold-warfare operations, later becoming a trainer for the CWO Course conducted during NOREX.

OPERATION NANOOK-NUNALIVUT

Team Leadership

Served as a team leader during Operation NANOOK-NUNALIVUT before subsequent transfer to Operation BOXTOP.

JTFN // OPERATION LIMPID

Hostile-Entity Contact

Transferred to Joint Task Force North for participation in Operation LIMPID. During the operation, Cardinal encountered a hostile entity later designated [REDACTED].

OPERATION LIMPID // INCIDENT REPORT

Extraction Cover

After the entity was terminated, an internal observer noted Cardinal's willingness to assume direct risk. Cardinal held off the [REDACTED] long enough for the remainder of the team to extract safely. Following the incident, Cardinal was officially discharged and transferred for Foundation recruitment and training.

FOUNDATION // GENERAL SECURITY

Operative Advancement

Quickly established a reputation as a capable operative. Cardinal rose through the General Security ranks while repeatedly demonstrating a willingness to place personal safety below the survival of assigned personnel.

MSG T // CALLSIGN ASSIGNMENT

"Radegast"

Upon reaching MSGT, Cardinal selected the callsign Radegast, traditionally associated with the concept of a protector.

1LT // SITE-66 TRANSFER

GENSEC Site Assignment

Upon promotion to 1LT, Cardinal received transfer orders to Site-66 and joined the site's General Security Department.
