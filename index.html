Previous History

Before training as a SCP Foundation General Security Operative, Radegast underwent training as a member of the Canadian Armed Forces. He subsequently specialized in cold-warfare operations, eventually becoming a trainer for the CWO Course held during NOREX.

Radegast served as a team leader during OPERATION NANOOK-NUNALIVUT. Following completion of the operation, he was transferred to OPERATION BOXTOP, and subsequently assigned to JTFN (Joint Task Force North) for participation in OPERATION LIMPID.

During Operation LIMPID, Radegast's unit encountered a hostile entity later identified as [REDACTED].

Following termination of the entity, an individual within the Canadian Armed Forces took note of Radegast's performance, specifically his willingness to place himself in direct danger to ensure the survival of his team.

Radegast remained behind to hold off the [REDACTED] while the remainder of his team successfully extracted.

Shortly afterward, he was officially discharged from the Canadian Armed Forces and transferred to another facility, where he began training as a Foundation operative.

Foundation Training & Service

During his Foundation training, Radegast quickly established himself as a highly capable operative. His performance resulted in assignment to the General Security Department.

He progressed through the ranks while becoming increasingly recognized by his superiors for his repeated willingness to place himself at risk in order to ensure the safety of personnel operating alongside him.

When questioned regarding this behavior, Radegast responded:

"To Not become like those we fight."

Radegast eventually reached the rank of MSGT and selected the callsign 'Radegast', a name traditionally associated with the concept of a protector.

Upon reaching the rank of 1LT, Radegast received transfer orders to Site-66.

Upon arrival, he was formally assigned to the Site-66 General Security Department, where he currently serves in a leadership capacity.
